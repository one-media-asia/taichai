Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 4, 2026
Service: Tai Chi Week Planner (taichi.onemedia.asia)
Operator: One Media Asia (one-media-asia)

This Privacy Policy explains how the Tai Chi Week Planner progressive web app (“the App”) handles information. The App is a practice planner for Yang-style Tai Chi.

1. Summary

  • We do not require an account to use the App today.
  • Practice progress, language choice, reminder settings, and install-banner preferences are stored on your device (browser local storage).
  • We do not currently use advertising trackers or analytics SDKs in the App.
  • If you enable reminders, your browser may ask permission to show local notifications on your device.
  • Hosting and delivery of the website files are provided by our hosting provider (currently Vercel).

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following in your browser’s local storage (it stays on your device unless you clear site data):

  • Practice progress — completed days, completed exercises, last active date, and demo step-speed preference.
  • Language — English or Simplified Chinese preference.
  • Reminder settings — whether reminders are enabled and the reminder time you chose.
  • Install banner — whether you dismissed the “install as app” prompt.

Clearing your browser data for this site, or resetting progress in Settings, removes that information from the device.

3. Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders and grant notification permission, the App may schedule a local reminder on your device. Notification permission is optional. We do not use this to send marketing messages.

4. Network and hosting

When you open the App, your device requests the App’s files from our hosting provider. Like most websites, standard technical data (such as IP address, browser type, and request timestamps) may be processed by the host’s infrastructure and logs for security, reliability, and delivery. We do not use that to build a marketing profile in the App itself.

5. Payments (planned)

We plan to offer an optional one-time unlock (for example, before full use or install prompts). If and when payments are enabled, payment card details will be processed by a payment provider such as Stripe — not stored by the App on your device. That provider’s privacy policy will also apply to payment data. This section will be updated when checkout goes live.

6. Children

The App is a general wellness/exercise planner and is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a future feature that collects it, contact us and we will delete it where applicable.

7. Your choices

  • Change or clear language and reminder settings in the App.
  • Use Reset All Progress to clear practice history stored locally.
  • Revoke notification permission in your browser or system settings.
  • Uninstall the home-screen / PWA shortcut and clear site data to remove local App storage.

8. International visitors

The App may be accessed from different countries. Hosting and related infrastructure may process technical data in the United States or other regions where the provider operates.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as the App changes (for example, when payments launch). The effective date above will be revised when we do. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Privacy questions about Tai Chi Week Planner: contact One Media Asia via onemedia.asia or open an issue on the project repository if you have access. If you prefer a dedicated privacy email, tell the operator and this page will be updated.
